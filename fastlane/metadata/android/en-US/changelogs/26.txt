New Sticker tool: draw around an object (or tap it) to cut it out with an outline and an optional shadow, then place it as a layer, open it as a new image or export it as a sticker. Object selection has smoother edges. The side sliders no longer jump when you grab the knob, and small brush sizes are easier to set. Fixed: a layer added after the app was restarted could get mixed up with an existing layer.
