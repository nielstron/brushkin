On-device AI tools, each downloaded once on first use and run on the device:
• Object selection is faster (GPU) and can be refined with Include/Exclude taps
• Remove Object fills in what was behind it
• Depth Blur keeps your focus point sharp and blurs the rest by distance
• Upscale ×2/×4, Denoise and Deblur
Tool list: favorites in a toolbar strip (long-press a tool), most-used tools first, a Filters group.
Transforms and crop work on the layer or the whole image. Aspect ratio setting for selections and crop.
Long operations show a progress bar and can be cancelled.
