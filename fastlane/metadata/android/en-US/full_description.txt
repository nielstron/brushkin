<p>A layered image editor for phones and tablets, inspired by GIMP and designed for fingers and styluses.</p>

<p><b>Insert:</b> photos, SVG, PDF and TIFF files, emojis, stickers and text.</p>

<p><b>Draw:</b> paintbrush, pencil, airbrush, ink and MyPaint-style brushes; smudge, clone, heal, dodge/burn; bucket fill, gradients, mirror and mandala symmetry.</p>

<p><b>Select:</b> rectangle, ellipse, lasso, fuzzy, by color, Intelligent Scissors and quick mask; tap an object to select it.</p>

<p><b>Transform:</b> move, scale, rotate, flip, shear, perspective, warp and cage, with guides, grid and snapping.</p>

<p><b>Colors:</b> curves, levels, hue-saturation, exposure, white balance, threshold and more.</p>

<p><b>Filters:</b> blurs, noise reduction, edge detection, lighting, distortions and artistic effects, all with live preview.</p>

<p><b>On-device AI:</b> cut out stickers, remove objects, blur the background by depth, upscale, denoise and deblur. Nothing leaves the device.</p>

<p><b>Layers:</b> groups, 17 blend modes, masks, editable text and non-destructive filters.</p>

<p><b>Tabs:</b> work on several images at once.</p>

<p><b>Files:</b> open and save GIMP .xcf and OpenRaster .ora; export PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF; send stickers to WhatsApp or Telegram.</p>

<p>Compositing happens in linear light on the GPU, like GIMP 2.10+, and all heavy work runs off the UI thread so the interface stays responsive even with large images.</p>

<p>The AI tools use open models (MobileSAM, MI-GAN, ZipDepth, PLKSR-Rep, NAFNet; Apache-2.0 and MIT). They aren't part of the app: each is downloaded once, after asking, when its tool is first used, and then runs on the device. The app contains only free software.</p>
