Brushkin builds these free-software components from source:
GEGL 0.4.62: LGPL-3.0-or-later core and GPL-3.0-or-later operations; https://gegl.org/
babl 0.1.114: LGPL-3.0-or-later; https://gegl.org/babl/
GLib 2.84.4 and json-glib 1.10.8: LGPL-2.1-or-later; https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/
GLib dependencies include PCRE2 10.44 (BSD), libffi (MIT), proxy-libintl (LGPL), and gvdb (LGPL).
Source archives, checksums, dependency revision, and Android build modifications are recorded in tools/build_gegl.py in the Brushkin source repository:
https://github.com/nielstron/brushkin
The dynamically linked libraries are included separately in the APK. License texts accompany this notice.
